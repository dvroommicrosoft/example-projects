Paris · 8e arrondissement

Place de la Concorde

A stylized, procedural study of the plaza at late afternoon: the Luxor Obelisk, the twin fountains, and the palace facades along the rue Royale.

  • Obélisque de Louxor — a roughly 23 m pharaonic monolith, raised on its pedestal at the centre.
  • Fontaines — a pair of basins to the north and south, here animated.
  • Hôtel de la Marine & Hôtel de Crillon — the colonnaded palaces closing the north side.

An artistic interpretation, not a survey.
