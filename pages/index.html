Example projects

Interactive demos with fictional data.

Teams Workbench

A Teams-style workspace with an ADX task board, linked threads and task details. Mention @Workbench in a thread to try the simulated bot. Light and dark themes follow your OS setting.

This is a standalone mock, not a live Teams or Workbench integration. Changes reset on reload.

Place de la Concorde

An artistic Three.js reconstruction of the Paris square with the Luxor Obelisk, fountains and surrounding facades. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.

A procedural approximation with original geometry, not a survey.

The repository also contains Tiny Triage, a Node application that requires a backend and is not served by this static site.
